# Using KnoTime

Open `dist/KnoTime.app`, or drag the app from `KnoTime.dmg` into Applications. Click the clock/city in the menu bar. Clicking outside dismisses the panel; reopening the app in Finder also opens the panel.

- Drag the timeline, or scroll over it, to compare times. Release the drag to snap back to now. A wheel gesture snaps back shortly after the gesture stops.
- Previous/next-day labels are relative to your computer's calendar date at the selected instant, and sit below a fixed-position time capsule.
- Click a city's briefcase to include its working hours. A soft green fill inside the entire timeline shows one city's work interval, or only the intersection when several cities are selected. There is no separate status row. No selection shows no fill.
- Right-click the briefcase to open the working-hours card directly (or use the city context menu). Enter local 24-hour start/end times. A range such as 22:00–06:00 spans midnight. Ranges repeat daily, including weekends.
- The sun/moon and yellow/purple capsules follow calculated sunrise/sunset at each city's coordinates, including during previews.
- Search offline, then click a result or press Return. Chinese aliases are included for the featured cities. Other available cities come from the bundled IANA time-zone locations.
- Click a name to rename; Return saves, Escape or leaving the field cancels. Drag rows to reorder. The context menu also offers Move Up/Down and Remove. Removal has a six-second Undo.
- With no cities pinned, a custom orbit-clock template icon appears in the menu bar and adapts to light and dark backgrounds.
- The eye toggles menu-bar visibility. The chevron reveals Date, 24 Hours, Compact View, Open at Login, and Quit.
